Our Mission

At Staarbridge, we are committed to empowering underprivileged kids in Columbus, Ohio by providing essential education on practical life skills that are often overlooked in traditional schooling. Our goal is to equip these young individuals with the knowledge of financial literacy and their legal rights, helping them navigate the complexities of adulthood with confidence.
